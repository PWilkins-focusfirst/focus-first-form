## Talking points (calls, not documents)

- Why $2,500: it prices the work, not the value — the work-up regularly finds coverage and defendants the file didn't know about. The credit means a client who goes forward pays nothing extra for it: research plus focus group costs the same $5,000 in fees as the focus group alone.
- Why now: in May 2026 the Supreme Court held — unanimously — that brokers can be sued for negligent carrier selection (*Montgomery v. Caribe Transport II*). Chains that were untouchable two years ago are reachable today, the § 14916 fights and network-agency rulings are still moving, and amendment deadlines punish lawyers who look at the chain late.
- What it is not: not a records-retrieval service, not a SAFER printout, not a demand-package mill. It is FocusFirst's intelligence discipline pointed at the chain above the driver.
