# BIR Intake Checklist — Materials Protocol

Source of truth for (a) the Phase 0 intake inventory and (b) the client-facing intake checklist document (regenerate the docx from this file; keep the two in sync). The client-facing version carries the same tiers in client-friendly language, FocusFirst branding, no work-product header (it is marketing/onboarding collateral, not case work product).

## The intake principle

The work-up runs on partial data. Tier 1 opens the engagement; everything else improves it. A missing document is never a reason to wait — it becomes a Truth Ledger row and a Pressure Campaign ask, which is often more valuable to the client than the document itself. Tell clients: **send what you have; the gaps are our job.**

## Tier 1 — Opens the engagement (items 1 and 3 have FocusFirst-retrieval alternatives; only the four anchors — carrier identity, date of loss, geography, venue — truly block, and those get one ask to Paul)

1. **Crash/police report** (or report number + date + location, and FocusFirst retrieves it — crash-report-retrieval)
2. **Date of loss** — drives every date-of-loss reconstruction
3. **Defendant carrier identity** — legal name and USDOT/MC if known; door placard text and unit/plate/VIN if not
4. **Crash location and direction of travel** — the geography anchor
5. **Venue / jurisdiction** — filed or anticipated court; controlling state
6. **Case posture** — pre-suit / filed / discovery open; limitations date and any amendment deadline
7. **Client contact** — the lawyer who owns the case and how they want the deliverable

## Tier 2 — High-value (send if it exists)

8. **Load paperwork of any kind** — bill of lading, rate confirmation(s), lumper receipts, proof of delivery, load/tender history (triggers the double-broker screen at full power)
9. **Complaint / answer** (if filed) and any scheduling order
10. **Discovery served and responses received** — especially carrier responses about brokers, leases, and "third parties"
11. **Insurance disclosures** — certificates, dec pages, MCS-90, any tender/denial letters
12. **Driver information** — name, CDL state, employer-of-record, any statements
13. **Photos/video** — door placards, unit numbers, trailer markings and trailer ownership, dashcam, scene
14. **ELD/telematics, driver qualification file, Part 376 lease** — if any early production exists
15. **Known or suspected shipper / load origin-destination** — even a hunch focuses Phase 1

## Tier 3 — Helpful context

16. Prior counsel or in-house work product on liability (memos, timelines)
17. Any OSI/pattern research already done
18. Correspondence with defense counsel or insurers
19. Client's working theory and what they most want the work-up to answer

## Explicitly NOT needed

- Medical records, bills, damages documentation — BIR is a liability and accountability work-up; damages work lives in the core FocusFirst engagement.
- Full document productions beyond the categories above (if a large production exists, send it — it will be serialized — but do not wait to assemble it).

## Intake protocol (FocusFirst side)

1. Inventory arrivals against tiers; log in `bir-engagement-log.md` (received / missing / retrievable-by-us).
2. Confirm the four anchors (carrier identity, date of loss, geography, venue). Ask Paul once if any anchor is missing; otherwise proceed.
3. Gap-fill what FocusFirst can retrieve without the client (crash report, FMCSA identity resolution, UCC-1s, docket pulls).
4. Serialize anything over ~25 pages (litigation-document-serializer) before analysis.
5. Confirm the engagement email went out before work began, and fee status ($2,500 — invoiced/paid) and target delivery date in the log.
